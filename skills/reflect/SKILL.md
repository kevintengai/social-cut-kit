# Reflect

Open The Saved Edit At The Revision Being Reviewed. Record The Project, Revision, Timeline Frame Rate Or Timebase, Intended Audience, Viewer Action, And The Review Surface. Use The Brief To Decide Which Questions Matter. Mark Missing Inputs As Unknown.

## Review A Cut

1. Play The Opening Through The First Delivery Of The Headline's Promise. Record The Time When A First-Time Viewer Can Identify The Topic. Compare The Spoken Claim With The First Frame And The Evidence Shown On Screen.
2. Replay Speech Cuts With The Original Audio. Listen Across Both Sides Of Each Suspect Boundary For A Missing Consonant, A Clipped Breath, A Level Jump, Or A Word Repeated Across Takes. Mark The Clip And The Timeline Range. Check Source Handles Before Choosing A Trim.
3. Inspect The Edit At Its Intended Display Size. Check Captions Against The Actual Speech, Timing Around Cuts, And Text Collisions With Platform Controls. Trace The Final Invitation To The Actual Offer And Delivery Path In The Brief.
4. Record Each Actionable Observation As A Time Range, A Direct Observation, Its Effect On The Intended Viewer Action, One Specific Edit, And A Repeatable Acceptance Check. Rank By Consequence. Keep Effective Moments With Their Evidence So A Later Edit Can Preserve Them.
5. If Audience Analytics Exist, Record Their Source, Date Range, Sample Size, And Metric Definition. A Drop At A Timestamp Generates A Hypothesis. Identify What You Would Check In The Edit Before Assigning A Cause. When Only The Script Or Transcript Is Available, Limit Findings To What That Material Shows.

## Editor Handoff

| Timeline Or Source | Direct Observation | Effect | Revision To Try | Acceptance Check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Example, 00:04.10 To 00:04.45 At 30 FPS, Seconds With Decimal Fractions | The Caption Changes To The Next Sentence While “School” Is Still Audible. | The Keyword Disappears Before The Viewer Hears It. | Move The Caption Boundary To The End Of “School.” | Play At Normal Speed And Confirm The Whole Word Is Audible And Visible Together. |

State The Strongest Observed Moment, The Highest-Priority Revision, And Any Untested Hypothesis Worth Following Up. Reopen The Saved Edit After A Fix And Replay The Original Range. Mark It Resolved Only When Its Acceptance Check Passes. Keep Kevin's Review In The Editor And Wait For His Personal Go-Ahead Before Any Video Export.
