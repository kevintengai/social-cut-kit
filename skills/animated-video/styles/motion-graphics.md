# Motion Graphics

Three Related Modes Built From Clean Shapes, Type, And Precise Timing. A Flat Infographic Turns Quantities And Processes Into Shapes. Kinetic Type Makes The Words The Picture. A Product-Interface Film Redraws An Interface To Show The Product Working. Choose This Look In [Style Frames](../STYLE-FRAMES.md). The Drawing Helpers Are Described In [Code Scenes](../CODE-SCENES.md).

## What Makes It Read As Designed

- **A Grid.** Margins, Columns, And One Base Unit That Every Position And Size Follows.
- **Restraint.** A Small Palette, One Stroke Weight, One Corner Radius.
- **Choreography.** Elements Move In Relation To Each Other. One Leads And The Others Follow.
- **Varied, Deliberate Timing.** Duration And Easing Are Chosen For Each Move From Its Distance, Its Size, And Its Importance.

## Layout

- Define The Grid Before Drawing. Keep The Platform's Own Buttons And Text In Mind And Leave Those Edges Clear At The Delivery Aspect Ratio.
- Draw Icons To One Set Of Rules. The Included Icons And Marks Can Serve. New Ones Should Match Their Stroke And Corners.
- Give Each Frame One Focal Point And A Clear Order Of Importance. Size Steps Should Be Large Enough To See.

## Easing And Timing

- Entrances Decelerate. Exits Accelerate. Moves Within The Frame Ease At Both Ends. Keep Linear Movement For Things That Are Mechanical, Such As A Conveyor Or A Progress Bar.
- Use Stronger Curves Than A Gentle Default. A Quick Start And A Long Settle Reads As Confident.
- Scale Duration To The Move. At 30 FPS A Small Element Can Arrive In Six To Ten Frames, And A Large Move Across The Frame May Need Twelve To Twenty. Treat These As Starting Points.
- Stagger Related Elements By A Few Frames, Led By The Most Important.
- Add Overshoot And Settle Only In A Playful Register. A Film Where Everything Bounces Has No Emphasis Left.
- Move One Idea At A Time. Two Unrelated Things Moving Together Split The Eye.

## Continuity

- Transform Elements Instead Of Clearing The Stage. A Bar Becomes A Line And The Line Becomes An Arrow. Each Reset Costs The Viewer Their Place.
- Let An Element Exit In The Direction The Next One Enters.
- Cut On A Match Of Shape, Position, Or Color. A Shape Can Grow To Become The Next Background.

## Kinetic Type

- Choose One Family With Several Weights, Or Two Faces With Clear Contrast. Use Installed, Licensed Fonts.
- Bring Words In By Phrase, On The Timing Of The Voice From [Voice Timing](../VOICE-TIMING.md). Word-By-Word Is A Choice For A Rhythm That Needs It, Not A Default.
- Emphasize The Stressed Word By Size, Weight, Or The Meaning Color. One Emphasis Per Phrase.
- Break Lines By Sense. Keep The Reading Order Obvious.
- Animate Text Only On The Way In And The Way Out. While It Is Being Read It Stands Still.
- Tighten The Spacing Of Large Type And Loosen Small Type. Keep Rotation To A Few Degrees.

## Infographic

- Encode Quantity By Length Or Position First. Use Area With Care And Avoid Volume.
- Draw Shapes In True Proportion To The Figures. Start Bars At Zero.
- Let The Data Arrive In The Order Of The Argument, So The Comparison Builds In Front Of The Viewer.
- Label Marks Directly. A Legend Makes The Eye Travel.
- Carry One Message Per Chart. Put The Mark That Matters In The Meaning Color And Mute The Rest.
- Count A Number Up Only When The Counting Is The Point. Land On The Exact Figure And Hold It. Always Show The Unit.
- Use The Chart Parts In [Parts](../PARTS.md). Every Figure Traces To A Source The Owner Supplied.

## Product Interface

- Redraw The Interface Instead Of Recording It. Keep The True Layout And The Real Labels Of The Elements That Matter. Reduce Everything Else To Plain Blocks.
- Enlarge The Control In Use. Push The Camera To The Active Region Instead Of Showing The Whole Screen Small.
- Move The Cursor Or Touch Point With Easing, And Answer Every Click With A Visible Response.
- Type Text At A Readable, Slightly Uneven Pace. Hold The Result State.
- Add A Device Frame From [Parts](../PARTS.md) Only When The Device Matters To The Point.
- Stay Truthful. The Redrawn Interface Does What The Real Product Does. Work From The Owner's Captures, And Add No Feature, Figure, Or State That Does Not Exist.
- When The Viewer Needs Proof More Than Clarity, Use A Real Capture. The Video Edit Skill's [Screen Demo Guide](../../video-edit/styles/screen-demo.md) Covers That.

## Color, Light, And Texture

- Use One Background, One Or Two Neutrals, One Primary, And One Accent With A Fixed Meaning From [Directing](../DIRECTING.md).
- Check Contrast For A Phone Used Outdoors.
- Keep Fills Flat. Use A Soft Shadow Only To Lift A Card, In One Direction And At Low Strength. Glow Belongs To [Luminous 3D](luminous-3d.md).
- Add A Very Fine Grain Over Any Gradient So It Does Not Band After Compression.

## Camera And Frame Rate

- Use A Flat Camera That Pans And Scales Across One Large Layout. Keep Positions Meaningful, So That Pulling Back At The End Reveals The Whole System.
- Render Smooth Motion At 30 Or 60 FPS With A New Position On Every Frame.
- Give A Fast Move Enough Frames To Be Seen, Or Draw A Stretched Smear Shape Across The Gap.

## The Quality Bar

- Any Paused Frame Sits On The Grid With One Focal Point.
- The Motion Leads The Eye To The Right Place Without Help From The Voice.
- No Two Unrelated Things Move At Once.
- Text Reads At Phone Size For As Long As The Voice Needs It.
- Shapes Are In Proportion To Their Figures.
- The Interface Behaves As The Real Product Does.

## Typical Failures

| Failure | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| A Template Look | Design Each Transition From The Content. Vary Duration And Easing By Distance And Importance. |
| Everything Bounces | Remove Overshoot Except Where The Register Is Playful And The Beat Is Important. |
| Motion That Means Nothing | Remove Loops And Wiggles. Every Move Is An Event Tied To A Beat. |
| Captions Passed Off As Kinetic Type | Give The Words Hierarchy, Phrase Timing, And One Emphasis. |
| A Chart That Misleads | Start At Zero And Draw True Proportions. |
| A Tiny Interface | Frame The Active Region. |
| The Stage Resets On Every Beat | Carry Elements Forward And Transform Them. |
| Mixed Strokes, Corners, And Colors | Return To The Style Sheet. |
